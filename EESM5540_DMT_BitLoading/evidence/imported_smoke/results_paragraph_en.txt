The author's simplified DMT digital-baseband Monte Carlo simulation uses Hermitian mapping, unitary IFFT/FFT, a cyclic prefix, causal FIR convolution, and receiver-side real Gaussian noise. Independent QPSK blocks estimate the channel and residual electrical symbol SNR; these estimates and the selected profiles remain frozen during payload testing. Uniform and loaded modulation share all 24 independent subcarriers, mean symbol energy, sampling rate, channel, and noise variance. This smoke run checks execution and output only; its counts are excluded from the formal three-seed statistics and cannot establish target compliance. Uniform and loaded profiles carry 48 and 120 bits per DMT symbol, corresponding to 42.6667 and 106.667 Mbit/s; their observed bit-weighted BERs are 0 and 4.16667e-05. No formal performance conclusion is drawn from this short run. Error bars are approximate 95% pointwise Wilson intervals, not simultaneous coverage; zero errors do not establish zero true BER. Payload rates include cyclic-prefix time but exclude initialization, feedback, and FEC overhead. This electrical FIR model omits optical-device physics and is not an optical hardware experiment or a complete Chow-algorithm reproduction.
